FORMULA 1

Formula 1 (F1) is the pinnacle of global motorsport, managed by the FIA. 
The championship features 24 races spanning five continents, with 11 teams and 22 drivers competing for both the Drivers' and Constructors' World Championships.
Formula 1 cars are single-seater, open-cockpit racing vehicles designed for extreme speed and handling.
They weigh about 798 kg (including the driver) and are powered by a 1.6-liter turbocharged V6 hybrid engine that delivers nearly 1,000 horsepower, allowing them to reach top speeds exceeding 375 km/h.
